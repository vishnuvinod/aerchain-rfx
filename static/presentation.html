Core Engineering Principle

Perception vs. Arithmetic Separation

Slide 4 of 8 · Technical Mechanics
The Golden Rule: Large Language Models (LLMs) are probabilistic and hallucinate arithmetic. In enterprise procurement, a ₹10 Lakh calculation error is legally binding. We never allow an AI language model to perform math.
AI Perception Layer (Gemini 3.6 Multimodal) Semantic & Vision
  • ✓ Optical Vision OCR: Reads handwriting and wrinkled smartphone rate card photos.
  • ✓ Footnote Extraction: Locates buried rebate clauses on Page 3 of PDFs.
  • ✓ Unstructured Text Parsing: Extracts item rates from conversational Word paragraphs.
  • ✓ Questionnaire Auditing: Reads ISO certificates, warranties, and SLA terms.
Outputs clean, structured key-value tokens (No calculations).
Deterministic Python Calculation Engine 100% Math Guarantee
  • ✓ FX Conversion: Applies official RBI benchmark rate (e.g. ₹83.50/USD).
  • ✓ Packaging Multiplier: Exact ratio math (100m spool × 3.05 = 305m master box).
  • ✓ Volume Rebate Math: Applies explicit 12% discount to net unit rates.
  • ✓ Spend Aggregations: Sums, ranking algorithms, and IEEE-754 financial precision.
Guarantees 100% repeatable, auditable arithmetic.
Architecture: Probabilistic AI extracts data; Deterministic Python calculates spend. Aerchain RFx Intelligence

1. Forced Vendor Portals: Suppliers resist logging into custom portals. Ingesting native formats gets 100% response rate.
2. Autonomous PO Dispatch: At ₹4.5 Cr spend, full autonomous release creates legal risk. System advises; human director signs.
3. Heavy Microservices: Single FastAPI + reactive Alpine.js stack provides zero cold-start latency and works offline.
Pragmatic Product Management: Solving the high-friction core.
